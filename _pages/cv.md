Research Profile
======
My research focuses on AI and intelligent data analytics for complex energy systems, particularly fault early warning, performance prediction, system modeling, and optimization. Current application areas include photovoltaic-battery microgrids, wind-turbine structural monitoring, catalytic hydrogen microreactors, cokemaking processes, and embedded intelligent systems.
