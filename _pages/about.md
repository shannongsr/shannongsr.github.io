I am a PhD student in the Faculty of Engineering at Monash University. My research lies at the intersection of smart manufacturing, hydrogen safety and utilization, machine vision, multimodal learning, CFD-machine learning hybrid modeling, and AI for Science. I am supervised by Prof. [Jianglong Yu](https://research.monash.edu/en/persons/jianglong-yu).

Before joining Monash, I completed a bachelor's degree in Electronic Information Engineering at Fuzhou University, where I developed a strong interest in deep learning, signal processing, embedded AI systems, image processing, and robotics-oriented engineering applications.

My recent work covers multimodal fault detection, domain adaptation, edge vision, robotic path planning, intelligent inspection systems, and energy-related process modeling. To date, I have published 10 SCI papers, including 5 first-author or corresponding-author papers, and I hold 6 authorized Chinese invention patents.

I am particularly interested in building research that combines physical understanding with deployable AI methods for real industrial and engineering systems. This includes interpretable modeling, robust cross-domain learning, and intelligent decision-making in complex environments.

I also maintain long-term collaborations with researchers in digital twins, medical AI, industrial modeling, and intelligent robotics.
